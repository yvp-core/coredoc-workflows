# Learning card

Keep a persisted learning under 120 words.

- **Lesson:** The reusable conclusion, stated concretely.
- **Applies when:** Scope and trigger conditions.
- **Evidence:** A stable commit SHA, path, test, incident, or measurement.
- **Do:** The future action this evidence supports.
- **Avoid:** The failure pattern this replaces.
- **Revalidate when:** The condition that may make the lesson stale.
- **Confidence:** `high`, `medium`, or `low`, with one short reason.
